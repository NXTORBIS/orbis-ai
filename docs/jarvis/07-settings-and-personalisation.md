## Voice
Enable speech output · Auto-read Jarvis replies · **Speak on dashboard load** · Language (English (US)) ·
Jarvis voice (System default) · **Speaking rate / Pitch / Volume** sliders · **Preview voice** / **Stop**.

## Audio & Voice — a whole audio engine
> "A consistent Jarvis audio language — subtle by default, memorable when it matters. **Most interactions stay
> silent.**"

- **Audio Engine: On**; **Master Audio** modes: Mute all · Voice only · Sounds only · **Do Not Disturb** ·
  **Reduced audio**.
- Channels, each switchable: **Voice Feedback · Interface Sounds · Achievement Sounds · Notification Sounds ·
  Focus Audio · Ambient Audio**.
- **Voice characters:** Jarvis Classic · Jarvis Calm · Jarvis Professional · Jarvis Technical · Jarvis Minimal.
- **Sound theme:** Classic · Soft · Bright · Deep.
- **Volumes:** Master 80% · Voice 85% · Effects 70% · Notifications 60% · Ambient 40%.
- **Voice tuning** (0 = personality default): Voice Rate, Voice Pitch.
- **Test sound · Test voice · Test achievement · Stop voice**, plus an **Audio Activity** meter.

## Keyboard shortcuts — the full map (with Restore Defaults)

**Search:** Global Search `Alt+Shift+K` · Focus Search `Alt+Shift+R` · **Command Palette `Ctrl+K`**

**Navigation:** Go Back `Ctrl+Alt+←` · Go Forward `Ctrl+Alt+→` · Dashboard `Alt+Shift+D` · Messages `Alt+Shift+M` ·
Files `Alt+Shift+O` · Notes `Alt+Shift+E` · Tasks `Alt+Shift+T` · Calendar `Alt+Shift+G`

**AI:** Open AI `Alt+Shift+A` · **Voice Mode `Ctrl+Alt+V`** · New AI Chat `Ctrl+Alt+C`

**Learning:** Academy `Alt+Shift+L` · Knowledge Base `Ctrl+Alt+J` · Notebooks `Ctrl+Alt+B`

**Studio:** Studios `Alt+Shift+I` · Chemistry `Ctrl+Alt+H` · Games `Ctrl+Alt+G` · Computer Science `Ctrl+Alt+S`

**Productivity:** Toggle Sidebar `Alt+Shift+B` · **Toggle Focus Mode `Alt+Shift+F`** · Projects `Ctrl+Alt+P` ·
Goals `Ctrl+Alt+Y` · Habits `Ctrl+Alt+U` · Focus Suite `Ctrl+Alt+F`

**Notes/Tasks/Files:** New Note `Ctrl+Alt+N` · New Task `Ctrl+Alt+T` · Links `Ctrl+Alt+L`

**Classroom:** Classroom `Alt+Shift+C` · Flashcards `Ctrl+Alt+D`

**Media:** Play/Pause `Ctrl+Alt+Space` · Mute `Ctrl+Alt+M`

**General:** Show shortcuts `Ctrl+/` or `?` · Close modal/panel `Esc` · Save `Alt+Shift+S` ·
**Submit/Run `Ctrl+Enter`**

## Custom cursor — an entire subsystem

Cyber · Ultra Minimal. **Size presets:** Tiny · Small · Medium · Large · XL.

**Live preview** ("move cursor here") against a Button, a Link and a Card.

**Trail** · **Click Effects** · **Movement & Idle** · Label · Advanced.

## Danger zone

---

## For Orbis

Orbis has: theme, accent, visual effects, voice settings, shortcuts. It does **not** have: assistant personalities,
a global accent applied to the orb, an audio engine with channels and sound themes, a custom-cursor system, Amoled,
an export-everything button beside a reset, or an anonymous-inbox feature.
